CadetX Unity Data Protocol

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Protocol

Official Protocol for NCC Cadets, Staff & Officers

1. Introduction

CadetX Unity is a dedicated digital community platform designed for the NCC fraternity, including active cadets, ex-cadets, eligible NCC staff, Associate NCC Officers and authorized Commissioned Officers. CadetX Unity is designed around the principles of Unity, Discipline, Authenticity, Safety and Responsible Digital Communication.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CadetX may collect, why that information is required, how it is used, how verification information is protected, and the choices and rights available to users.

CadetX Unity is an independent digital platform unless otherwise expressly stated. References to NCC ranks, appointments, camps, units, wings or organizational structures are used for community identification and verification purposes and do not by themselves indicate that CadetX is an official Government of India or NCC platform.

2. Separate Registration & Verification Process

To maintain an authentic and trusted community, CadetX does not use exactly the same registration inputs for every category of user. The information requested depends on the user's role within the NCC community.

A. Cadet / Ex-Cadet Registration
  • Full name & Username / CadetX handle
  • Email address & Profile photograph
  • NCC Regimental Number, where applicable
  • Wing (Army, Naval or Air), Division & Directorate
  • Battalion / Unit, Rank & Cadet status
  • Camp participation, Achievements & Certifications
B. Officers & NCC Staff Registration
  • Full name & Designation (ANO/GCI/PI STAFF/CTO/JCO/Officer)
  • Email address & Profile photo
  • NCC Unit (Optional) & Rank
  • Verification criteria matching official eligibility

3. CadetX Verification System

CadetX uses a structured verification system to reduce impersonation, fraudulent accounts and misuse of NCC-related identities (Unverified, Green Badge for Verified Cadets, Blue Badge for NCC Staff, Golden Badge for Commissioned Officers, and Red Crest for Platform Admin).

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Account Information

Name, Username (CadetX Handle), Email, Rank, Profile photograph.

4.2 NCC & Community Info

Regimental Number, Wing, Division, Directorate, Battalion/Unit, Rank, Achievements.

4.3 Verification Info

Photograph in full uniform with clear face, nameplate and rank visible. Restricted strictly to audit.

4.4 User Content & Technical

Posts, photos, videos, stories, comments, DMs, IP address, device info, security logs.

5 & 6. How We Use Information & Verification Document Protection

CadetX uses information to verify authenticity, maintain security, deliver official announcements, enable Direct Messaging, and operate unit spaces. We do NOT use verification information for commercial profiling.

Verification Document Protection Protocols:
  • Restricted access limited exclusively to authorized verification personnel.
  • Zero public display of verification documents on user profiles.
  • Strict exclusion from indexing by public web search engines.
  • Retention only as long as reasonably necessary for operational and audit security.

7. Direct Messaging & Communication Safety

CadetX Unity provides a Direct Messaging (DM) feature for eligible users to communicate privately. Responsible Use Policy: DM must NOT be used for harassment, threats, spam, fraud, impersonation, sharing confidential/classified military data, or private info without consent.

Communicate responsibly. Respect privacy. Protect the community.

8 – 13. Platform Operations, Security & Account Binding

8. Official Notifications: Notification bell is reserved for official platform and unit security broadcasts.
9. Zero Data Sale: CadetX does NOT sell, rent, or trade cadet emails, phones, or regimental records to advertisers.
10 & 11. Security & Binding: HTTPS/TLS encryption, database access controls, account binding via Google + Mobile + Regimental ID.
12 & 13. Content & Stories: Classified military data strictly prohibited. Stories expire automatically after 24 hours.

14. Children's & Minor Cadet Safety

Because some NCC cadets may be minors, CadetX enforces strict protection measures including anti-harassment protocols, controlled Direct Messaging, rapid account reporting, moderation, and zero tolerance for improper contact or manipulation.

15 – 17. User Rights, Data Retention & Category Privacy

User Rights: Access, Correction, Profile Update, Account Deletion, Grievance, and Withdrawal of Consent. Data is retained only as long as required for active services, security logs, or legal audit requirements.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

CadetX Unity may update this Privacy Policy when platform technology, legal standards, or processing practices change. Significant updates will be published with an updated effective date.

20. Important User Responsibility

CadetX Unity is intended to be a trusted community for the NCC fraternity. Every member is expected to protect their own information and respect the privacy of others.

Do not share confidential, classified, operationally sensitive or unauthorized information on CadetX.
